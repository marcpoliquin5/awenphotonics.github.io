Publication controls

Claims begin with reproducible artifacts.

AWEN software checks establish build, test, schema, and interoperability behavior. They do not establish physical-device speed, energy, accuracy, scaling, or advantage.

Current evidence status

This site publishes no AWEN benchmark comparison and attributes no quantitative result to third-party research. Earlier paper characterizations and technology forecasts were removed because the adjacent wording was not reliably supported.

Required before a hardware claim

  • A versioned workload, fixture, backend identity, calibration input, and stopping rule.
  • A source commit and immutable raw artifact produced by the repository benchmark workflow.
  • An explicit baseline, tensor or problem dimensions, repetitions, tolerances, and failure accounting.
  • End-to-end timing and energy boundaries including host transfer, optical source, converters, calibration, and digital post-processing where applicable.
  • A generated public claim that links back to the content-addressed artifact.
